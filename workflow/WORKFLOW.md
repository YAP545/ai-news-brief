# AI News Brief Workflow
1. Collect: search each source group separately (company blogs, tech press, business/policy, HN/Techmeme, India). Keep items from the last 24-36h.
2. Deduplicate: cluster by event (company + action + day); pick one canonical source (primary > wire > best secondary); list others as "also covered by"; drop rewrites.
3. Rank: Impact 35%, Novelty 20%, Breadth 20%, Source quality 15%, Momentum 10% (each 1-5). Penalty -1 for rumor/single-source, opinion, funding under ~$50M without strategic angle. Keep top 8-10.
4. Write: Top story, Models & Research, Products & Companies, Policy & Regulation, Funding & Deals, Worth watching. Paraphrase only, 400-600 words, label unverified claims.
5. QA: dates in window? duplicates? every item has link + source? single-source claims labelled?
